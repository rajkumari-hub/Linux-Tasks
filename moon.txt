The Moon is Earth's only natural satellite.
It revolves around the Earth and is the fifth-largest natural satellite in the Solar System.
The Moon orbits the Earth.
It takes about 27.3 days to complete one orbit around the Earth.
The Moon does not produce its own light.
It appears bright because it reflects sunlight.
The Moon's gravity affects Earth.
Its gravitational pull causes ocean tides and influences Earth's rotation.
The Moon has no atmosphere.
Since it has almost no atmosphere, there is no weather, wind, or rain on the Moon.
The Moon has many craters.
